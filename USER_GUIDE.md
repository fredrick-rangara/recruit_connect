# RecruitConnect User Guide

Welcome to RecruitConnect! Follow these steps to get the most out of the platform.

### 1. Getting Started
*   **Sign Up**: Click the **Sign Up** button in the navbar.
*   **Select Role**: Choose **"Job Seeker"** if you are looking for work, or **"Employer"** if you are looking to hire.
*   **Login**: Use the **Login** link if you already have an account. Forgot your password? Use the link in the login form.

### 2. Finding & Applying for Jobs (Job Seekers)
*   **Search**: Use the filters on the **Home** page (Location, Category, Experience Level) to find the perfect role.
*   **View Details**: Click the **"View Details"** button on any job card to read the full description and requirements.
*   **Apply**: On the Job Details page, click **"Apply for this Position"**. Fill in your **Resume URL** and a **Cover Letter**, then hit **"Send Application"**.
*   **Save Jobs**: Click **"Save Job"** on the details page to keep track of interesting roles without applying immediately.

### 3. Managing Your Career (User Dashboard)
*   Go to **Dashboard** in the navbar to see:
    *   **Your Applications**: Review all the jobs you've applied to and check their status (Pending, etc.).
    *   **Saved Jobs**: Quick access to roles you are still considering.

### 4. Hiring Talent (Employers)
*   **Post a Job**: Go to your **Dashboard** and click the **"+ Post New Job"** button. Fill out the title, description, and salary to list it on the Home page.
*   **Review Applicants (ATS)**: On your **Dashboard**, you'll see a list of recent applicants. Click **"Review Submission"** to see their resume and cover letter.
*   **Company Profile**: Click on your company name in any of your job listings to view your dedicated **Employer Profile** page.

### 5. Account Management
*   **Profile**: Click **"Profile"** in the navbar to view your account details.
*   **Logout**: When you're done, click **"Logout"** to secure your session.
